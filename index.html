How Unseal Works — 100% Anonymous Scratch Notes

Unseal (unseal-cards.vercel.app) is a zero-server digital scratch-off note maker crafted for heartfelt confessions, anonymous crush notes, Hinglish inside jokes, bestie roasts, and dares. Unlike anonymous apps that store messages on servers or demand signups, Unseal packs your recipient name, secret message, optional burn setting, and clue inside a compact browser-side link.

Unseal defaults every note to realistic Champagne Gold metallic foil and automatically adapts typography—using expressive Kalam calligraphy whenever Devanagari Hindi is detected and warm Caveat script for English and Hinglish notes.

Ephemeral 60-Second Self-Destruct & Haptic Scratching

Enable Burn 60s after unseal before sealing a confidential confession. Once your recipient rubs away 45% of the Champagne Gold foil with crisp haptic feedback, a live countdown ticks down from 00:59s to 00:00s and dissolves the secret forever.

Instant WhatsApp Reactions & Free 9:16 Story Card

After revealing the note, receivers can tap a 1-tap WhatsApp reaction pill, seal a secret note back in 10 seconds, or download a high-resolution 1080×1920 warm-linen Story PNG with zero share gates.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or account?

Unseal compresses your recipient name, message, clue, and burn setting directly inside your browser into a short URL. No backend database or account ever reads or stores your note.

2. Is sending a secret card on Unseal 100% anonymous?

Yes. Unseal never asks for your name, phone number, email, or login. You choose how much hint—if any—to include inside the optional Clue box.

3. How does the Burn 60s self-destruct timer work?

When Burn 60s after unseal is enabled, a live 00:59s countdown begins as soon as the receiver scratches 45% of the gold foil. At 00:00, the secret dissolves cleanly.

4. Can receivers react or download a Story Card without unlock hoops?

Yes. Right after unsealing, receivers can send a 1-tap WhatsApp reaction, seal a secret note back in 10 seconds, or freely download a 1080×1920 9:16 Story PNG.

5. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis cleanly?

Yes. Unseal automatically detects Hindi Devanagari characters and switches to Kalam handwriting, while English and Hinglish render in warm Caveat script.

6. Are older Unseal links (?seal=, ?s=, U3, and #4) still supported?

Yes. Unseal remains backward compatible with legacy ?seal= and ?s= links alongside U3, #4, and #5 short links.
