How Unseal Works — Zero-Server Privacy & Bespoke Gift Cards

Unseal (unseal-cards.vercel.app) is a zero-server digital scratch-off note maker crafted for heartfelt confessions, anonymous crush notes, Hinglish inside jokes, wishes, apologies, and dares. Unlike anonymous messaging apps that store conversations on remote servers or require signups, Unseal encodes your recipient’s name, message, custom foil & font styling, and clue locally in your browser into an ultra-short link.

Every sealed note uses compact in-browser text packing with Devanagari Hindi support so links stay short, trustworthy, and reliable across WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Telegram, and SMS on every phone.

Why Every Mystery Story Gift Card Feels Personal

Both sender and receiver get their own instant Surprise Story Gift Card. One tap reveals a bespoke 1080×1920 Story PNG card featuring custom procedural vector geometry, rich palette tones, a Vibe Aura score, and a fortune line—zero note spoilers included.

Custom Styles, Self-Destruct Option & Installable App

Choose from Champagne Gold, Rose Gold, Sterling Silver, and Holo Prism foils, 4 handwriting styles, and 12 selectable ambient themes (with automatic date awareness), toggle an optional 60-second self-destruct timer, and install Unseal straight onto your home screen from Chrome, Edge, or Safari.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or account?

Unseal compresses your recipient name, message, styling, and optional clue inside your browser into a short URL format. Zero servers or databases ever read or store your note.

2. Do both sender and receiver get unique Mystery Story Gift Cards?

Yes. Each sender and receiver can crack open their own unique 9:16 Keepsake Gift Card in one tap—no locked share hoops—ready to download for WhatsApp Status or IG Story.

3. Can I install Unseal on my phone directly from the browser?

Yes. Supported mobile & desktop browsers automatically prompt native installation and Unseal provides an in-app Install button, offline Service Worker, and Add to Home Screen steps for Safari.

4. Can I change themes, foil finishes, and fonts?

Yes. Pick any of the 4 metallic scratch foils and 4 handwriting ink fonts directly in the composer, and tap Style in the header to explore all 12 curated themes or leave Auto calendar mode active.

5. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis cleanly?

Yes. Unseal pairs Caveat script for English with Kalam and Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi handwriting, keeping shared links ultra-compact across Android and iPhone.

6. Are older Unseal links (?seal=, ?s=, U3, and #4) still supported?

Yes. Unseal remains 100% backward compatible with legacy ?seal= and ?s= links alongside U3, #4, and styled #5 short links.
