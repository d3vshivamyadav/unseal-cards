How Unseal Works — Private, Zero-Server Scratch Notes

Unseal is a lightweight digital scratch-off postcard maker built for honest confessions, inside jokes, birthdays, apologies, and midnight thoughts. Unlike messaging bots or anonymous inbox platforms that save conversations inside third-party cloud databases, Unseal encodes every letter directly inside your browser using compact binary packing and lossless client-side URL compression.

Every sealed card receives a clean, short link showing the recipient’s name alongside a randomly generated 8-letter card pass ID, making it effortless to share via WhatsApp, Instagram Direct, Telegram, or SMS. Native support for UTF-8 and 1-byte Devanagari packing means both English calligraphy and Hindi handwritten messages stay crisp, compact, and fast across iPhone, Android, and desktop browsers with zero sign-up required.

Why Share Your Personalized 9:16 Status Gift Card?

Whether you just sealed a note for someone special or finished scratching a mystery postcard open, Unseal unlocks a collectible 9:16 Name Gift Card designed specifically for WhatsApp Status and Instagram Stories. Tips for Writing Memorable Scratch-Off Confessions & Notes

The best digital scratch notes feel personal, warm, and slightly mysterious. If you are messaging a college crush, mention one specific shared memory—like a rainy afternoon chai break, an inside joke from class, or their playlist—paired with a playful clue so guessing becomes half the thrill. For best friends, birthdays, or overdue apologies, sincere one- or two-line notes in conversational English or handwritten Hindi carry far more weight than long paragraphs. Before hitting seal, always double-check the recipient’s name so your personalized Status Gift Card and postcard greeting look effortlessly thoughtful across every Android and iPhone screen, then paste your short link anywhere friends can tap and unseal it cleanly. Whether opened in Chrome, Safari, or an in-app social browser, your card scales automatically without awkward zooming.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or account?

Unseal compresses your recipient name, message, and optional clue directly inside your browser into a short URL combining the recipient name, an 8-letter unique card ID, and a compact encrypted fragment. Zero servers or databases ever read or store your secret.

2. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis inside secret notes?

Yes. Unseal pairs Caveat script for English with Kalam and Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi, and packs Devanagari characters into a single byte per character so Hindi, Hinglish, and emoji notes stay extra short and render crisply on every phone.

3. How do the personalized Status Gift Cards work?

When you seal and share a secret note—or finish unsealing one—Unseal unlocks a personalized 9:16 Name Gift Card designed for Instagram Stories and WhatsApp Status without exposing private note spoilers to everyone viewing your status.

4. Can the recipient automatically see my name?

If you enter your name in the optional Gift Card field, it stays strictly local to customize your own Status Gift Card and is never attached to the sealed link. Only the optional clue you write is shown below the scratched card.

5. Does the scratch foil work properly on iPhone and Android browsers?

Yes. The HTML5 canvas uses touch-action locking and passive-false touch listeners so mobile pages never scroll while rubbing the foil. Once 45% of the foil is scratched off, the note reveals smoothly with warm confetti and gentle chime audio.

6. Are older Unseal links (?seal= and ?s=) still supported?

Yes. Unseal remains backward compatible with legacy ?seal= and ?s= links alongside the shorter ?Name-UniqueId link format.
