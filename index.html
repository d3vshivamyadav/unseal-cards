How Unseal Works — Private Scratch Cards & Mystery Gift Vaults

Unseal is a lightweight, zero-server digital scratch-off note maker crafted for heartfelt confessions, anonymous crush messages, Hinglish inside jokes, apologies, and fun dares. Unlike anonymous inbox apps that upload conversations to remote cloud databases or demand account sign-ups, Unseal packs your recipient’s name, message, and optional clue right inside your browser into an ultra-compact encrypted URL fragment.

Because every sealed postcard uses our custom 6-bit phrase encoder paired with 1-byte Devanagari packing and an 8-letter unique pass ID (such as ?Maya-QbQCdbtD#...), links stay noticeably shorter, cleaner, and friendlier to tap inside WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Telegram, and SMS—showing a rich gold-foil preview card every time you share.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or account?

Unseal compresses your recipient name, message, and optional clue inside your browser into a short URL format combining the recipient name, an 8-letter card pass ID, and a compact client-side encoded fragment. Zero servers or databases ever read or store your note.

2. How do both sender and receiver get a Mystery Story Gift Card?

Both the sender after sealing a note and the receiver after scratching it open receive a locked 2026 Mystery Collector Vault. Sharing Unseal with 5 friends on WhatsApp cracks open all five wax seals to reveal their randomized Luxury Rarity Pass, Vibe Score, Fortune Prophecy, and spoiler-free 9:16 PNG for Instagram Story or WhatsApp Status.

3. Can I install Unseal on my phone like a native app?

Yes. Unseal is a full Progressive Web App (PWA) with an offline Service Worker and manifest. Tap Install App in the top header on Android Chrome, Edge, or desktop—or use Share then Add to Home Screen in iPhone Safari—to launch it fullscreen from your home screen.

4. Why are Unseal links shorter and safe to tap on WhatsApp?

Unseal v7 uses 6-bit phrase packing and 1-byte Devanagari compression so typical notes compress into ultra-compact URL fragments alongside a readable recipient name slug (?Name-8LetterId), avoiding sketchy-looking redirect shorteners.

5. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis cleanly?

Yes. Unseal pairs Caveat script for English with Kalam and Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi handwriting, keeping Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis crisp on both Android and iPhone screens.

6. Are older Unseal links (?seal=, ?s=, and U3) still supported?

Yes. Unseal automatically decodes legacy ?seal= and ?s= links as well as U3 and compact U4 URLs.
