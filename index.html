How Unseal Works — 100 Unique Gift Designs & Zero-Server Privacy

Unseal (unseal-cards.vercel.app) is a zero-server digital scratch-off note maker crafted for heartfelt confessions, festive wishes, anonymous crush notes, Hinglish inside jokes, apologies, and dares. Unlike anonymous messaging platforms that save conversations inside remote servers or demand logins, Unseal encodes your recipient’s name, message, and clue right inside your browser into an ultra-short link.

Every sealed postcard combines a 6-bit phrase encoder with 1-byte Devanagari packing and an 8-letter pass ID (?Maya-QbQCdbtD#...) so links stay compact, reliable, and clean inside WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Telegram, and SMS.

Why No Two Collector Mystery Gift Cards Look the Same

Every sender and receiver receives their own locked Mystery Gift Vault backed by 100 pre-composed Collector Editions spanning 20 luxury palettes and 5 geometric artwork engines (Mandala Rays, Art Deco Sunburst, Aurora Ribbons, Celestial Constellations, and Royal Heritage Arch). Sharing Unseal with five friends on WhatsApp cracks open all five wax seals to unblur your randomly rolled #001–#100 edition and export a crisp 1080×1920 PNG with zero note spoilers.

Automatic Indian & Worldwide Festival Themes + Installable PWA

From Diwali, Holi, Eid Mubarak, Navratri, Rakhi, and Pongal across India to New Year, Valentine’s Week, Spring Blossom, Halloween, and Christmas worldwide, Unseal’s theme engine shifts ambience, floating sparkles, and celebration confetti cleanly to match the season—and runs as a full-ratio, offline-ready PWA installable straight from your browser.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or account?

Unseal compresses your recipient name, message, and optional clue inside your browser into a short URL format combining the recipient name, an 8-letter card pass ID, and a compact client-side encoded fragment. Zero servers or databases ever read or store your note.

2. Do both sender and receiver get unique Mystery Story Gift Cards?

Yes. Each sender and receiver randomly rolls 1 of 100 unique Collector Mystery Gift Card designs (#001 to #100) with custom geometric artwork, palette, Vibe Aura score, and fortune prophecy. Completing 5 WhatsApp shares unlocks the 1080×1920 9:16 PNG for Instagram Stories and WhatsApp Status.

3. Can I install Unseal on my phone directly from the browser?

Yes. Unseal prompts supported mobile and desktop browsers cleanly via PWA install and includes an offline Service Worker, fullscreen shell, and Add to Home Screen instructions for iPhone Safari.

4. How do the Indian and Worldwide Festival themes work?

Unseal automatically adapts colors, ambient floating sparkles, festival banners, confetti, and gift card accents based on major Indian and global festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Navratri, Rakhi, Pongal, New Year, Valentine’s Week, Cherry Blossom, Halloween, Christmas), plus lets you switch any theme manually.

5. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis cleanly?

Yes. Unseal pairs Caveat script for English with Kalam and Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi handwriting, and uses 1-byte Devanagari compression so links remain crisp and short on every Android and iPhone.

6. Are older Unseal links (?seal=, ?s=, and U3) still supported?

Yes. Unseal remains 100% backward compatible with legacy ?seal= and ?s= links alongside shorter U3 and U4 ?Name-8LetterId links.
