How Digital Scratch-Off Secret Notes Work (Zero-Server Privacy)

Ever wished you could drop a handwritten folded chit on someone’s desk—without risking awkward screenshots on a random confession app? Unseal brings back that nostalgic stationery magic for WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and Stories using pure client-side URL encoding. Instead of saving your crush confession, 2 AM thought, or inside joke inside a third-party database, your browser turns the entire note into a compact, UTF-8 safe URL token (?seal=...).

Chahe aap English mein likhein, Hinglish mein dil ki baat bolein, ya Hindi Devanagari aur emojis use karein, your message never touches a server log or requires an account. Jab aapka friend link open karta hai, unki screen par ek realistic brushed silver ya champagne gold foil postcard load hota hai. Only when they rub off 45% of the foil with their finger or mouse does the handwritten Caveat note unseal with warm paper confetti.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers on URL-based privacy, mobile touch foil, Hinglish & emoji support, and sharing on Instagram or WhatsApp.

1. How does Unseal send secret scratch-off notes without a server or login?
Every character you type—recipient name, mood stamp, secret note, foil finish, and optional sender clue—is serialized locally inside your browser into a UTF-8 URL-safe token attached to the ?seal= parameter. Because all data lives inside the link itself, Unseal never uploads your confessions to any backend server, database, or third-party cloud.
2. Does Unseal support Hindi, Hinglish, and emojis inside secret notes?
Yes! Standard browser Base64 functions break on Unicode characters, so Unseal converts text with TextEncoder UTF-8 bytes first before encoding. That means Hindi Devanagari, Hinglish, Urdu, accented characters, and all modern emojis render flawlessly on every phone and laptop.
3. How do I share my secret scratch card on WhatsApp or Instagram Stories?
Tap Seal with Silver Foil & Get Link to lock your card. From there, tap Send Secret Card on WhatsApp to open a ready-to-send message with your link, use Share via... for your phone’s native share sheet, or click Copy Link for Instagram Story / DM to drop it into a Story link sticker or DM.
4. Can the recipient find out who sent the secret note?
Unseal does not track or embed your name, phone number, email, or IP address inside the link—the card only shows the Sender Clue you type. However, remember that messaging apps show who sent a message: if you want true mystery, share through a story link, anonymous group drop, or play along with a fun clue.
5. Does the scratch-off foil work smoothly on iPhone, Android, and desktop?
Yes. The interactive HTML5 Canvas uses touchmove with passive: false and touch-action: none so mobile browsers never scroll while your finger rubs away the foil. Desktop visitors can scratch using a mouse or trackpad. Once 45% of the foil is cleared, the remaining layer fades away automatically with a paper-confetti burst.
6. How can I save or post an unsealed note to my WhatsApp Status or IG Story?
After scratching off at least 45% of the foil (or tapping Quick Reveal), click Download 9:16 Story Card (PNG) directly under the postcard. Unseal renders a crisp 9:16 warm-linen postcard image with your revealed handwritten note and clue ready to upload straight to Instagram Stories or WhatsApp Status.
