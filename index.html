How Unseal Works — Zero-Server Privacy & Bespoke Gift Cards

Unseal (unseal-cards.vercel.app) is a zero-server digital scratch-off note maker crafted for heartfelt confessions, anonymous crush notes, Hinglish inside jokes, wishes, apologies, and dares. Unlike anonymous messaging apps that store conversations on remote servers or require signups, Unseal encodes your recipient’s name, message, and clue locally in your browser into an ultra-short link.

Every sealed note uses compact in-browser text packing with Devanagari Hindi support so links stay clean and reliable across WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Telegram, and SMS on every phone.

Why Every Mystery Story Gift Card Feels Personal

Both sender and receiver get their own locked Surprise Gift Vault. When five friends join via WhatsApp, the seals open to reveal a freshly composed 1080×1920 Story PNG card featuring custom vector geometry, rich palette tones, a Vibe Aura score, and a fortune line—zero note spoilers included.

Quiet Seasonal Styling & Installable App Experience
